My College

The CMR Groups in Kandlakoya, Hyderabad (Medchal Road) includes prominent institutions like CMR College of Engineering & Technology (CMRCET) and CMR Engineering College (CMREC). Established in 2002, they are UGC-autonomous, NAAC-accredited, and offer popular engineering, pharmacy, and management programs.Academia at the Kandlakoya campus focuses heavily on future-ready technology and industry alignment. It offers a wide array of undergraduate and postgraduate programs, with a heavy emphasis on Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) and its specialized branches like Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science. Beyond engineering, the campus hosts well-established programs in Management (MBA) and Pharmacy. The institutions utilize an autonomous curriculum that allows them to regularly update their syllabi to match evolving industry trends, ensuring students gain both theoretical depth and practical engineering skills.

The management is committed in assuring quality service to all its stake holders viz parents, students, alumni, employees, employers and the community. Commitment and dedication are merged into our policy of continual quality improvement by establishing and implementing mechanisms and modalities, ensuring accountability at all levels, transparency in procedures and access to information and services.
